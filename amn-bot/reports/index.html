Backtest and verification results

Engine, risk controller and test matrix are built and verified. The strategy has too little real data behind it to call it profitable or not.

What these results can and cannot say

All 3 real trades came from about 24 calendar days of 1-minute data (Yahoo Finance caps 1m history near 30 days). Three trades prove nothing about edge. The bot's logic is checked by the test matrix and by a no-edge control run, not by that sample.

Gold, S&P and Nasdaq use futures proxies (GC=F, ES=F, NQ=F). Spreads and slippage are simulated, there are no commissions, and the news calendar contains only US NFP releases.
