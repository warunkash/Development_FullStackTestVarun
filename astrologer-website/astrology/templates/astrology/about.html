About us

How we work

{{ site.name }} is a small practice. Four readers, one desk, and a preference for saying less rather than more. Most of our clients come to us at a decision point — a job, a match, a move — and what they usually need is a careful second perspective rather than a prediction.

What a session is like

You send your birth details and your question ahead of time, so the reader has already cast and studied your chart before you speak. The session itself is a conversation. You will be asked questions as often as you ask them. Afterwards you get a recording and a written summary, because almost nobody remembers the second half of a reading.

What we will not do

  • Predict a death, a diagnosis or a legal outcome.
  • Sell you a gemstone, a yantra or a puja as a condition of the reading.
  • Tell you a chart is cursed, or that a problem needs repeat sessions to clear.
  • Give advice that belongs to a doctor, a lawyer, a therapist or an accountant.

If a reader suggests a remedy, it will be something simple, optional and free or near-free. That is a house rule, not a preference.

Being straight about what this is

Astrology is a symbolic tradition with a long history and no established physical mechanism. We take it seriously as a framework for reflection and we are careful with it, but we are not going to tell you it is a science. Clients who get the most out of a reading treat it as a structured way of thinking about their own situation — which is, in our experience, where its real value sits.
