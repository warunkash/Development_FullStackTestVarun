How Financials Are Connected

Balance Sheet
Current & Long-Term Assets
+Cash
+Inventory
+Account Receivable
+ PP&E
+Intangible Assets
+ Others
= Total Assets
Liabilities
+ Account Payable
+ Short-Term Debt
+ Long-Term Debt
+ PP&E
+ Others
= Total Liabilities
Equity = Asset – Liabilities
P/L Statement
Revenue
- COGS
= Gross Profit
- Operating Expenses
- SG&A
= EBITDA
- D&A
= EBIT
- Interest Expenses
= Profit Before Tax
- Tax Expenses
= Profit After Tax
- Dividends
= Retained Earnings
Cash Flow Statement
EBIT
+ D&A
+ Other Non-Cash Expense
- Increase In Inventory
- Increase in Receivable
+ Increase In Payable
= Operating Cash Flow
- Net Interest Paid
- Tax Paid
= Net Operating Cash Flow
- Capital Expenditure
- Dividends
+/- Change in Equity
+/- Change in Debt
= Movement In Cash
